Getting Around

Tour like you're really here

  1. Look around

    Click and drag (or swipe on your phone) to spin a full 360°. Pinch to zoom in on the details.

  2. Follow the arrows

    Glowing arrows inside each scene walk you from the entrance to the lobby, upstairs and into a real room.

  3. Jump anywhere

    Open All Stops to skip to any hall or space, or search for things like "lounge" or "shower".

  4. Roll the dice

    Can't decide where to start? Let fate pick a lobby, lounge or room for you.
